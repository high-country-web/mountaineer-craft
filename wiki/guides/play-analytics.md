# Play Analytics

Mountaineer Craft uses Plan to collect player activity and provide player pages in a web dashboard. You can register for your own page at [analytics.mountaineercraft.net/register](https://analytics.mountaineercraft.net/register).

## Register for an Account

1. Join Mountaineer Craft with the Minecraft account whose activity you want to view.
2. Run `/plan register` in-game and follow the link or registration instructions it provides.
3. Complete the registration on the Plan website. If the site gives you a code, return to Minecraft and follow the displayed instructions to finish linking your account.
4. Sign in at [analytics.mountaineercraft.net](https://analytics.mountaineercraft.net/).

Plan's registration command can direct you to the registration page or complete a registration using a code. See the [Plan command reference](https://github.com/plan-player-analytics/Plan/wiki/Commands-%26-Permissions) for details.

## Open Your Player Page

Run `/plan player` in-game to get a link to your own player page. Your web account lets you view that page after signing in. Plan's page and available information may change over time.

## Analytics and Discord Are Separate

Plan analytics registration is separate from linking your Minecraft account to your website profile with the Discord bot's `/link` command. For that process, see [Discord Commands and Your Website Profile](discord-and-website.md).
