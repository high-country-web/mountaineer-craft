# Server Rules

Please follow these rules in Minecraft and in the Mountaineer Craft Discord community.

1. **Be respectful.** Treat everyone with respect. Harassment, hate speech, and discrimination are not allowed.
2. **Do not spam or flood chat.** Avoid excessive messages, emojis, capital letters, or repeated content.
3. **Keep content appropriate.** Do not post NSFW, offensive, or otherwise inappropriate content.
4. **Follow Discord's Terms of Service.** Follow Discord's Terms of Service and Community Guidelines when using the Discord community.
5. **Do not cheat or hack.** This includes clients or modifications that provide unfair advantages, such as X-ray or fly hacks.
6. **Do not exploit bugs.** Report bugs to staff instead of using them for an advantage.
7. **Respect builds and property.** Do not grief, steal from, or destroy another player's work.
8. **Do not create lag machines or abuse the server.** Avoid anything intended to cause lag or crash the server.
9. **Use Discord channels appropriately.** Keep conversations in the channels meant for them.
10. **Do not advertise without permission.** This includes other servers, services, or social media.

Rule violations may result in a warning, mute, kick, or ban depending on the situation.
